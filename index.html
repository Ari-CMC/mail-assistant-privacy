Personal Mail Assistant

A private tool used only by its owner to help manage their own email. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

The app accesses the owner's Gmail account only with the owner's permission, and only to help the owner read, organize and draft email.

  • Email data is used solely to assist the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
